## 👥 Squad Management in Practice

Squad management is the heart of the toolkit. A good squad view helps you see the whole picture instead of chasing one position at a time. Start by reviewing each area of the team: defense, midfield, and attack. Note where you have plenty of options and where a single injury could leave you short.

From there, you can plan roles for each player. A player who is strong in one role may be less effective in another, so it helps to match your squad to the way you want to play. The toolkit makes it easier to keep those decisions in one place instead of relying on memory.

## 🧠 Tactical Planning That Fits Your Squad

Tactics work best when they match the players available. Use the tactical planning area to record your preferred formation, the roles you want in each position, and the way you want your team to approach matches. When your squad changes, revisit the plan and adjust.

Having a written plan helps you avoid constant changes after every result. You can compare your tactical idea with the players you actually have and decide whether to adapt the plan or bring in different players.

## 🔁 Transfers and Scouting Together

Transfers and scouting belong together. Scouting helps you find players who fit your tactical plan, and the transfer planning area helps you decide which moves are worth making. Before a transfer window, review your squad needs and your scouting reports side by side.

Think about squad balance, not just individual quality. A talented player who does not fit your system can create more problems than solutions. The toolkit helps you keep those considerations visible while you plan.

## 🏋️ Training and Season Analytics

Training is where long-term improvement happens. Set priorities that support your tactical style and the areas where your squad is weakest. Regular review helps you see whether training is producing the progress you expect.

Season analytics give you the wider view. Instead of judging your club on one match, you can look at trends across many matches. That perspective makes it easier to decide whether to stay with your plan or make a change.

## 💡 Tips for a Better Experience

- Keep your club notes up to date so your decisions are based on current information.
- Review your squad after major changes, such as new signings or injuries.
- Use scouting reports before committing to transfers.
- Revisit your tactical plan when your squad changes significantly.
- Check analytics regularly rather than only after a bad run of results.
